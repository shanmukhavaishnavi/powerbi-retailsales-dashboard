# powerbi-retailsales-dashboard

Power BI Dashboard analyzing retail sales performance.

## Key Insights
- Identified the highest-performing product categories based on sales and profit.
- Analysed yearly business growth and highlighted performance changes across 2023-2025.
- Compared shipping methods to evaluate operational efficiency.
- Measured customer profitability to identify high-value customers.
- Visualised sales distribution across Indian states using map visualisations.
- Monitored product return rates and their impact on profitability.
- Tracked monthly profit margins to identify seasonal business trends.

## Project Highlights
- End-to-end Power BI Dashboard Development.
- Interactive Business Intelligence Reporting.
- DAX Measures & Calculated Columns.
- Data Cleaning & Transformation with Power Query.
- Geographic Sales Analysis.
- Customer & Product Performance Analytics.

## Business Value
This dashboard enables stakeholders to:
- Monitor business performance in real time.
- Identify top-performing products and regions.
- Evaluate customer purchasing behaviour.
- Improve profitability through return analysis.
- Support data-driven strategic decision-making.


## How to Use
1. Download `retail sales data.pbix`
2. Open it in Power BI Desktop
